# Publication review

## Reports

The reports are useful as supporting evidence, but not all generated reports
need to be part of a concise portfolio repository. The recommended public set
is the evaluation protocol, final readiness summary, model/evaluation summary,
and reports directly linked from `README.md`. Large exploratory reports,
duplicate CSV exports, and generated figures should remain local unless they
are specifically cited or needed to reproduce a claim.

Published metrics must retain their historical-benchmark qualification. The
reports document temporal shift, SZZ-derived labels, lack of prospective
intervention evidence, and that the score is not a calibrated probability.

## Presentations

The Markdown presentation and Mermaid diagrams are suitable supporting source
documents after checking that they do not expose local paths, raw records, or
unverified claims. The generated `.pptx` and `.doc` files are excluded by the
release allowlist because they are binary deliverables rather than required
runtime/project source.

## Current decision

No license is added by this review. No raw external dataset or bundled
third-party source is approved for staging. The root allowlist is the source of
truth for the initial public release review.
